1. PCB FABRICATION SPECIFICATIONS (Bare Board):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Board Dimensions: 70.00 mm x 70.00 mm
- Layer Count: 4 Layers (Multilayer FR-4 High-TG150, standard JLC04161H-7628 stackup)
  * L1 (Top Layer): Signal traces, SMT component pads, 50-ohm coplanar RF waveguides.
  * L2 (Inner Layer 1): Solid, uninterrupted GND reference plane (60.0 x 60.0 mm).
  * L3 (Inner Layer 2): Power planes (VBAT_PWR and +3.3V_SYS solid copper polygons).
  * L4 (Bottom Layer): Thermal GND plane & Keystone 1042/1048 18650 battery clip pads.
- PCB Thickness: 1.6 mm
- Copper Weight: 1 oz (35 um) Outer Layers
- Surface Finish: ENIG (Electroless Nickel Immersion Gold) - Required for Pogo-pin contacts
- Solder Mask Color: Green (or Matte Black)
- Silkscreen Color: White
- Minimum Track / Clearance: 0.20 mm / 0.20 mm (8 mil / 8 mil)
- Minimum Drill Hole: 0.30 mm
- Mounting Holes: 4x 3.20 mm (for M3 screws) at (5,5), (65,5), (5,65), (65,65) mm
- Milling Slot (Edge.Cuts): 2.0 mm routed L-slot separating Southeast corner (HV Stage)
  * Note: Creepage distance > 14 mm across slot for 5kV / 1000V galvanic isolation.

2. SMT ASSEMBLY SPECIFICATIONS (PCBA):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Assembly Type: Single / Double-sided SMT + THT (Top: SMT components, Bottom: Battery Clips)
- Assembly Quantity: 10 units
- BOM File: BOM_JLCPCB_CollarNet_V3_70x70.csv (contains exact LCSC Part Numbers)
- CPL File: CPL_JLCPCB_CollarNet_V3_70x70.csv (Pick & Place Mid X/Y and Rotations)
- Key ICs:
  * U1: ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8 (Espressif - LCSC C2913202)
  * U2: SIM7670SA-MNGV 4G LTE Cat-1 + GNSS (SIMCom - LCSC C2892994)
  * U3: SC7A20TR 3-Axis Accelerometer (Silan - LCSC C392477)
  * U4: CN3791 MPPT Solar Charger 6V (Consonance - LCSC C154992)
  * U5: SY8089AAAC 3.3V/2A Step-Down Buck (Silergy - LCSC C78988)
  * U8: EL817S1(C) 5kV Optocoupler (Everlight - LCSC C4999)
  * TH1: TYN1225RG 1200V/25A SCR Thyristor (ST - LCSC C14486)
  * BT1: Keystone 1042 / 1048 Battery Clips for 18650 Cell (Bottom Layer)
  * J1: Magnetic 4-Pin Pogo Connector IP68 (+5V, USB D-, USB D+, GND)
